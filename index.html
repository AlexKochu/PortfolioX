Product Strategy DPDP Compliance Consent-by-Design UX Architecture

Intrusive upfront consent banners cause severe user drop-off in gamified mobile apps under India's new DPDP Act regulations.
Product Framework
Designed "Progressive Trust"—requesting contextual consent gradually as users unlock specific social and leaderboard features.
Result & Prototype
Interactive prototype demonstrating a 0-dropoff onboarding flow while maintaining 100% statutory consent logging.
